About Maestro

Maestro is an AI-powered interior design studio. It turns an ordinary photo of a room into a photorealistic redesign while preserving the real architecture — walls, windows, doors and perspective — so what you see is what could actually be built. Designers, real-estate agents, home-owners and renovation teams use it to explore ideas and win approvals faster.
