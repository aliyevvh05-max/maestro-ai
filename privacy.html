Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026. Contact: maestroaisupport@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your name and email address provided by Google when you sign in; the room photos you upload and the images generated from them; basic technical data needed to run and protect the service (such as IP address in server logs); and purchase records (plan, amount, date). Card details are handled by our payment processor, Paddle; Maestro does not store card details.

How we use it

To provide renders, manage your account and credits, process payments, prevent abuse and provide support. We do not sell your data and do not use your photos to advertise to you.

Service providers

Your uploaded photo and the chosen style are sent to our AI provider (OpenAI) to generate the render. Payments are processed by Paddle. These providers process data under their own privacy terms. Our hosting provider stores the database and images.

Cookies

We use a single essential session cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and a script library from a public CDN, which may receive your IP address.

Retention and deletion

You can delete any generation from your gallery at any time, which removes the stored images. To delete your whole account and data, contact maestroaisupport@gmail.com. Payment records may be kept as long as required by tax and accounting law.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under Azerbaijani personal data law), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data and to object to processing. Contact maestroaisupport@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions use secure HTTP-only cookies and traffic is encrypted over HTTPS. No online service can be guaranteed completely secure.
